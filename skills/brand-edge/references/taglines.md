# Taglines

Use this workflow for a tagline, slogan, or short brand line. Reuse approved positioning, actual offer facts, voice, and constraints. A narrow tagline request does not require a new positioning exercise. Ask a focused question only when a missing answer would change the meaning or material promise. For a provisional exploration, label assumptions and use what the current offer can support.

## Choose the line's job

Distinguish a lasting brand tagline from a campaign line tied to one initiative and a call to action that tells the reader what to do. Use the requested purpose. State a brief assumption when its intended use is unclear.

Consider the line beside the brand and the context where buyers will see it. It can express a stance, experience, or identity without literally describing the product. Retain a precise technical advantage when it is the buyer's deciding reason. Use pleasure, aspiration, or practical value where relevant rather than manufacturing a painful problem.

Complete this step when the line's purpose, intended context, and supported strategic meaning are clear or explicitly provisional.

## Explore the useful approaches

Compare meanings before polishing wording. These approaches can prompt exploration, but do not define an exhaustive set:

| Approach | Useful question |
| --- | --- |
| Directive | What relevant action or attitude does this brand invite? |
| Experience promise | What experience can the offer actually deliver? |
| Reason to believe | Which supported capability gives buyers a concrete reason to choose? |
| Identity | Which self-description or aspiration do relevant buyers express? |

Use the approaches that fit, or another that better expresses the strategy. A different sentence shape is not necessarily a different idea. Evaluate candidates for understandable meaning in context, voice, memorability, relevance, and support. Check whether a close alternative could truthfully use the same line. Generic praise does little to express the chosen position; clever words alone do not establish competitive difference.

Match the requested quantity and format. When unspecified, deliver a compact shortlist of distinct candidates, recommend one, and explain the meaningful tradeoff briefly. For a lines-only request, return the lines without extra rationale. A requested word limit takes priority over a default preference for short lines, while material qualifications remain intact.

Complete this step when the candidates express distinct useful meanings, fit the requested job, and the recommendation has a reason beyond personal taste.

## Check support and deliver

Keep factual promises within the supplied evidence, including technical terms, delivery limits, comparisons, and experience claims. If a line needs an unadopted operational change, label it provisional beside the candidate. A proposed identity or creative expression is not an observed customer belief. Preserve the difference between sourced audience language and an illustrative line.

Treat new lines as proposed copy until approved. Report legal availability as unchecked unless the relevant searches and review actually occurred. When a user requests clearance, distinguish a preliminary search from qualified legal review.

For an audit of an existing tagline, use the scoped promise-audit method in [brand application](brand-application.md). Review only the supplied or inspected touchpoints and delivery evidence.

Finish when the proposed lines meet the purpose and format, important claims have support or a visible dependency, and their approval and clearance status is accurate.
