# Fantasy / xianxia / supernatural action

> **Lens note.** The camera stacks in this file are grade, stock and finish templates. Their millimeter values are legacy — convert the lens to a FOV degree from the ladder in `optics.md` before writing (`47° (50mm)`, `29° (85mm)`, `84° (24mm)`). Millimeters never lead, and apertures, ISO and lens brand names control nothing.


Spell effects, energy transformations, particle-driven VFX. Use as base genre for: xianxia / wuxia, magical realism, sci-fi action, supernatural beats, transformation sequences with VFX, spell-casting moments, hero-power reveals.

---

## Structural priorities (when used as base)

1. **VFX is the visual hero.** Spells, energy, transformations are the focal content. The character is the vector for the VFX.
2. **Stylized + grounded.** Photoreal environments often combined with stylized character VFX. Keep the world believable; let the powers be supernatural.
3. **Transformation arc.** Calm hero → power awakening → power deployment → power resolution. The arc is non-negotiable.
4. **Particle physics specificity.** Don't say "energy explodes" — say "energy detonates outward in concentric rings, debris vaporizing into glowing fragments spiraling upward."

## Verb register (Tier 4 / Tier 5 with VFX-specific vocabulary)

For action: SLAM, LUNGE, VAULT, BARREL, CRASH, ROCKET, DRIVE, STRIKE, RECOIL.

For VFX deployment: ERUPT, BURST, SURGE, PULSE, RIPPLE, SHIMMER, COIL, UNFURL, IGNITE, MANIFEST, SUMMON.

For VFX impact / climax: DETONATE OUTWARD, SHOCKWAVE OUTWARD, FRAGMENT, DISSOLVE INTO, SCATTER INTO, EXPLODE OUTWARD, COLLAPSE INWARD, IMPLODE.

For VFX dissolution: DISSIPATE, FADE INTO PARTICLES, WHISPER AWAY, SETTLE TO STILLNESS.

## VFX bracket density (heavy)

Fantasy genre uses VFX brackets as primary visual content. 3–5 brackets per 15-second scene is normal.

Examples:
- [VFX: golden energy ribbons spiraling up her body, runes igniting on her skin in a chain reaction]
- [VFX: blue lightning veins exploding across both forearms, sparks jumping between fingers]
- [VFX: shockwave detonating outward in concentric rings, debris suspended mid-air, energy strands trailing]
- [VFX: hero's body dissolving into glowing geometric shards, particles spiraling upward in a violent vortex]
- [VFX: massive energy beam discharge, blast punching through target, chunks exploding outward]

## Camera grammar defaults

- **Establishing:** wide low-angle for hero entrance, environmental drone descent for scope
- **Power-up beats:** medium close-up controlled handheld, camera tilts up to follow rising energy
- **Engagement beats:** mixed handheld + stabilized + lateral track, quick cuts on impact moments
- **Climax beat:** extreme wide pulled back to show scope of effect, often slow-motion
- **Resolution beat:** medium close-up locked-off on the hero, energy settling

Camera should feel premium-cinematic — not chaotic action / not handheld documentary. Controlled craftful camera that gets out of the way of the VFX.

---

## Lighting defaults

- Dramatic side-key with deep fill ratio
- Color contrast: cool environment, warm hero key
- Magical accent lighting (the energy itself becomes a light source)
- Volumetric atmosphere catching the energy effects
- Anamorphic flares on energy peaks

## Color grade

- Cinematic warm-cool dual register
- Hero color isolation (the energy color holds saturation)
- Deep preserved shadows
- Halation on highlights from energy
- Selective grain (cinematic film texture)

For Chinese / xianxia register: gold + jade green + crimson saturation, ink-wash shadow style, volumetric clouds.

For modern fantasy / sci-fi register: blue + magenta + white energy palette, neon accents, cool environment.

---

## Slow-motion patterns (heavy use)

Fantasy genre uses slow-motion as a primary tool:

> RAMPS TO SLOW MOTION as the energy ribbons spiral up her body, runes igniting frame-by-frame on her skin, the wind catching her hair in suspended motion, light reflecting off the rising particles. SNAPS BACK to real-time as she lands, both hands glowing fully ignited.

> RAMPS TO SLOW MOTION at the moment of contact — her palm meets the energy core, fragments suspended in air, light radiating outward in concentric rings. SNAPS BACK as the explosion completes, her body thrown clear of the impact zone.

Use slow-motion at: power awakening, peak impact, climax detonation, dissolution. Generally 1–2 ramp moments per scene.

---

## Sound design

Fantasy SFX is layered and dense:

```
SFX: deep low-frequency hum at power awakening, energy crackle and surge, rising chord on transformation, climax explosion with sub-bass impact, high-frequency particle tinkle on dissolution, return to environmental ambient at the resolution.
```

For Chinese xianxia: add gong tones, traditional instrument punctuation, wind through bamboo.
For sci-fi: add electrical zaps, plasma hum, mechanical whirs.

## Specific VFX patterns by element

### Lightning / electric
- White-blue current, fractal branching circuits, sparks jumping
- Crackling sound, ozone smell implied, hair standing
- Best for: tech-heroes, sci-fi, cyberpunk, summoner-types

### Fire / flame
- Orange-red ribbons, ember particles rising, heat-haze distortion
- Crackling sound, warmth on faces, smoke trails
- Best for: classic fantasy, dragon-themed, warrior-types

### Ice / frost
- Pale blue-white, crystal formations, frost spreading on surfaces
- Glass-like sound on formation, breath fog, cold-air shimmer
- Best for: northern fantasy, sorcerer-types, controlled magic

### Energy / pure light
- Neon bright (often green / blue / white), particle trails, refraction
- High-frequency hum, light bloom in environment
- Best for: sci-fi, modern fantasy, hero-power register

### Earth / stone
- Grey-brown debris, ground cracking, slabs rising
- Deep rumble, dust clouds, weight in motion
- Best for: classic fantasy, tank-types, defensive magic

### Wind / air
- Visible streamers, dust kicked up, hair blown
- Whistling tone, leaves and debris carried
- Best for: archer-heroes, agile fighters, support magic

Pick the element that matches the hero / brief and lean into its specific particle physics.
