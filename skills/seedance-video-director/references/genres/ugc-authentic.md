# UGC / authentic

> **Lens note.** The camera stacks in this file are grade, stock and finish templates. Their millimeter values are legacy — convert the lens to a FOV degree from the ladder in `optics.md` before writing (`47° (50mm)`, `29° (85mm)`, `84° (24mm)`). Millimeters never lead, and apertures, ISO and lens brand names control nothing.


Authenticity is the hero. Handheld phone aesthetic, grain, imperfection drive realism. Use as base genre for: user-generated-content style ad creative, testimonials, social media native content, mobile-first vertical video, "real person" reviews and demos.

---

## Structural priorities (when used as base)

1. **Imperfection is credibility.** Polished cinematic kills the UGC read. Grain, slight focus drift, off-axis framing, and natural light variability are features, not bugs.
2. **Vertical-friendly framing.** Default to 9:16 unless brief specifies otherwise. Compose for mobile portrait viewing.
3. **Talking-head or POV framing.** First-person handheld phone or front-facing camera selfie position. Subject is closer to lens than cinematic would prefer.
4. **Casual, immediate language.** When dialogue or VO is involved, use casual conversational tone with filler words preserved ("like," "honestly," "okay so").

---

## Style declaration line patterns

- "iPhone 15 Pro front-facing camera, natural daylight from a window, slight motion blur on movement, fine sensor grain, vertical 9:16 framing, casual handheld."
- "Authentic UGC handheld phone footage, slight overexposure on highlights, natural skin tones with no retouching, wood-grain warmth in the room light, vertical mobile aesthetic, 9:16."
- "Selfie-mode talking head, slight wide-angle distortion at frame edges (typical phone front camera), natural daylight from a window behind camera, soft skin rendering, fine grain, 9:16."
- "POV phone-mounted action camera, natural light, slight handheld instability, fine compression grain, color slightly muted (out-of-camera profile, no grade applied), 9:16 or 4:5."

---

## Verb register (Tier 2 with Tier 3 punctuation)

walk, hold, lift, show, point, gesture, tilt (the camera or the product), swipe, scroll, tap, pour, set down, turn, glance, react.

UGC verbs prioritize observable interaction. The user is showing the camera something (a product, a setup, a process). Verbs match.

For energy beats: snap (the camera turns suddenly to show something else), bounce (handheld settling after a quick move), drop (something hits the table for emphasis).

---

## Camera grammar defaults

- **Selfie / talking head:** front-facing phone camera at slight low-angle (looking up at face), arm-extended distance
- **POV product demo:** phone mounted or held over the shoulder, looking down at hands and product
- **Walking-and-talking:** phone held arm-extended, face in upper portion of frame, environment passing
- **B-roll:** quick handheld shots of products, environments, hand details — slight focus drift acceptable
- **Reaction:** zoom-in via cropping (digital zoom feel), face fills frame, eyes wide

UGC camera moves rarely — when it moves, it's a quick handheld correction or a deliberate "let me show you" tilt.

---

## Lighting defaults

- Natural daylight through a window (soft, slightly directional)
- Practical room lights visible in frame (lamps, ring light if creator-style)
- Slight overexposure on highlights (typical phone exposure behavior)
- Window backlight creating slight halation on hair / shoulders
- Indoor environments lit by motivated practical sources

Avoid: cinematic three-point lighting (kills the read), studio softbox setups, controlled gel lighting.

---

## Color grade

- Out-of-camera flat (slightly muted, no aggressive grade)
- Warm wood and skin tones held slightly warm
- Highlights slightly clipped (mobile sensor characteristic)
- Slight chromatic aberration at frame edges
- No film emulation, no Hollywood grade

The "ungraded phone footage" look. If the prompt has a heavy grade applied, it stops reading as UGC.

---

## Atmosphere modifiers

UGC scenes are usually clean of atmospheric particulate. Use sparingly:

- Steam from a hot drink in the foreground
- Dust motes in window light (subtle)
- Window condensation on a cold day
- Slight breath fog in cold environments

Avoid: smoke, fog, theatrical particulate. UGC environments are domestic / casual.

---

## Sound design

- Natural ambient room tone
- Phone audio quality (slight compression, occasional pop)
- The creator's voice if there's narration / talking head
- Background sounds NOT removed (background music from another room, distant traffic, kids in another room — this is texture, not noise)

---

## Common UGC scenes

- Product unboxing on a kitchen counter, phone held overhead by one hand while the other unwraps
- Creator talking head review, phone propped against a coffee mug, casual lighting
- Walking-and-talking selfie from a coffee shop, phone arm-extended, environment passing in background
- Quick demo at a desk, phone propped to show hands working with the product
- Reaction-style "wait, what?!" with sudden zoom-in on creator's face
- Day-in-the-life style cuts of casual moments

---

## Composition with other genres

### As modifier on `commercial-product`
- Cranks authenticity for product reviews / testimonials
- Strips cinematic polish, leans into "real person actually using this"
- Useful for B2C / DTC brands that want trust without polish

### As modifier on `viral-hook`
- Makes the hook feel native to the platform
- TikTok / Reels register where polish reads as advertising

### As modifier on `explainer-clarity`
- Casual instructional content
- "Here's how I do it" tutorials
- Personal-voice explainers

---

## What this genre does NOT do

- Cinematic camera moves (cranes, dollies, polished pulls)
- Three-point studio lighting
- Color grades that read as commercial post-production
- Voice-over narration in TV-commercial register
- Wide cinematic framing (UGC is portrait-format intimate)

---

## Negative prompt block

```
Negative: no professional studio lighting, no cinematic color grade, no smooth gimbal motion, no commercial-quality polish, no watermarks, no subtitles, no text overlays except creator-style on-screen text.
```

---

## Vertical framing considerations

When 9:16 is specified, write for vertical composition:

- Subject is centered vertically, with environment above and below
- Action happens within the vertical frame, not across the horizontal
- Faces fill the upper third or center, hands and product in the middle third, environment below
- Avoid wide horizontal action — it gets cropped on mobile autoplay

Mention 9:16 explicitly in the prompt's style line and in the Total footer.

---

## Examples of "real" UGC moments

- "She holds the phone in front of her face, smiles awkwardly, says 'okay so I tried this thing,' tilts the camera down to show the product on the desk."
- "He stands in his kitchen, phone propped against a coffee maker, opens the package on the counter while talking, slight glare from the window behind him on the surface."
- "POV looking down at her hands as she opens a box, the box logo blurred (not the focus), her painted nails partially visible, a coffee mug in the lower edge of frame."

These read as observed authenticity. Polished narration and theatrical performance kill the register.
