# Physics — cause and effect

Floaty, weightless, rubbery motion is the most recognizable AI-video tell. It comes from describing what a body does without describing the mass doing it. Motion needs cause and effect: contact, weight transfer, follow-through.

Add physics language where the diagnosis flagged it — weight, impact, weapons, falls, running, liquids, particles. Not every prompt needs a physics pass. A locked-off portrait does not.

---

## What to enforce

gravity · mass · inertia · friction · contact · weight transfer · ground pressure · collision · follow-through · cloth delay · hair delay · liquid viscosity · particle accumulation · heat shimmer · vehicle mass · hinge resistance · weapon weight

---

## By category

**Walking** — heel contact, weight transfer through the foot, hip shift, toe push-off, body mass settling on each step.

**Running** — real ground contact, knee lift, opposing arm swing, torso lean into the direction of travel, varied stride length. Speed in km/h.

**Weapons and heavy objects** — the arm carries visible weight, the wrist angle reacts to the mass, the object has inertia, the motion accelerates and decelerates. The object never teleports between poses.

**Impact** — build slow, hit fast, aftermath slow. Name the direction of force, not the destruction sequence — the engine handles the destruction if it has the vector.

**Liquids** — blood and water cling, drip, smear, pool, stain, and follow gravity. Droplets travel in parabolic arcs. Wet contact leaves visible residue. Flow has viscosity and direction.

**Snow, smoke, fire, dust, particles** — particles move with a stated wind direction, exist in foreground, midground and background when atmosphere matters, accumulate on surfaces over the runtime. Hot air meeting cold creates shimmer. Density in %, visibility depth in meters.

**Cloth and hair** — motion arrives late relative to the body and settles after the body stops. Name the delay when the movement is fast enough to show it.

**Vehicles** — mass expressed through suspension travel, weight shift on turn-in, tire deformation, body roll. Speed in km/h.

---

## Pressure fracture

When cracks, breaks, or debris need to happen *without* an impact point — glass fracturing from thermal stress, a wall giving under crowd density, ice splitting from cold:

- describe the origin as edge stress or slow pressure, never a point of strike
- move the fracture pattern from edge inward rather than radiating from a center
- time the progression asymmetrically: 0.3s at one edge, 0.6s spreading, 1.0s meeting the midline
- for crowds: *the crowd pushes forward as a mass under its own weight, no strike, no throw*

---

## Measurables Seedance reads

- **Speed** in km/h — vehicles, running figures, pans, tracking moves. Never fast, slow, quick.
- **Atmosphere** in % density plus meter visibility — "haze 30%, readable to 40 meters." Never light fog, smoky.
- **Scale** by stacking humans — "as tall as three humans standing on each other's shoulders." Never huge, enormous.
- **Direction** always from the lens — "moves screen-left" means left from where the camera is.
- **Emotion** in muscle — jaw sets, breath quickens, knuckles blanch. Never sad, angry, afraid without a body cue.
- **Environmental contact** rendered physically — snow gathering on the shoulder, wind lifting hair ends, rain darkening fabric, breath condensing at exhale.

---

## The floor test

Read the motion back and ask what is holding the body up and what it is pushing against. If there is no answer, the body will float.
