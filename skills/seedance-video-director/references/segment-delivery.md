# Segment delivery

Use this reference when the requested duration exceeds the verified limit of the selected frontend. The engine notes provide a starting point if the frontend is not known; label the resulting segment length provisional.

Split the piece at an action or story boundary rather than compressing the entire narrative into one short call. Provide one independent paste-ready prompt per segment.

For each segment, specify:

- The runtime and aspect ratio in the external specification.
- The supplied references needed for that segment.
- The opening frame, active action, and final frame.
- The continuity shared with adjacent segments.

Describe the end state in concrete terms such as subject position, gaze, prop contact, camera framing, and light direction. The following segment begins from that state. Preserve wardrobe, palette, and aspect ratio unless the brief calls for a change.

Explain that the user generates the segments separately and assembles them in an editor. Any required end-frame image is a dependency to capture or create, not an attachment that already exists. List those dependencies separately from the confirmed upload manifest.
