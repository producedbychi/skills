# Membership and community: Spotify Wrapped 2025

[See Spotify's campaign examples and outdoor images](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-12-03/wrapped-marketing-campaign/).

**Offer and visual mechanism.** Wrapped makes a listener's own year in music the subject. Its visual-mixtape treatment turns personal listening data into something worth sharing, while outdoor placements and fan installations bring that identity into public space. The asset is not a generic “join now” card; it shows what belonging feels like and what members can claim as theirs.

**Adapt when:** a membership produces a real personal record, milestone, access moment, or community identity. Feature the member's meaningful artifact or experience. Use a real value signal, not a decorative dashboard of invented numbers.

**Requires:** verified data and consent for any identifiable member story. Without member data, use a truthful example marked as illustrative. Spotify reports the reach and scope of Wrapped as a campaign, but the article does not isolate the conversion effect of one image.
