# Local business: Debbie & Sons in Canva's UK campaign

[See the sandwich-shop story and its campaign visuals](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/uk-brand-campaign-2025/).

**Offer and visual mechanism.** The campaign starts with an ordinary local sandwich shop whose product is easy to picture. New menus, branding, and social posts make a business transformation tangible. The sandwich stays recognizable, while designed touchpoints explain what changed. This card is about the local-business subject; the [software card](software.md) examines the same campaign from the tool seller's side.

**Adapt when:** a service improves the way a local business is seen or experienced. Show the actual storefront, service moment, menu, team, or customer setting. A clear environmental cue can do more than a floating product bag, generic stock portrait, or invented rating.

**Requires:** real business assets for any factual before-and-after claim, or a clearly fictional concept. The example is a staged brand campaign, not proof that the depicted sandwich shop or an individual ad achieved a measured sales lift.
