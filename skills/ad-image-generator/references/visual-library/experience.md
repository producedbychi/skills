# Experiences: Expedia's “Stuff” campaign

[See Expedia's description and the published spot](https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/in-game-day-ad-expedia-and-ewan-mcgregor-promote-experiences-over-stuff/).

**Offer and visual mechanism.** The commercial moves through sets advertising things, then exits to a beach. The visual turn embodies the message that a trip can matter more than another purchase. For a static ad, the transferable idea is a legible contrast: the crowded alternative versus the feeling or place the offer unlocks.

**Adapt when:** the offer is a trip, event, hospitality stay, or other experience whose value is hard to put on a tabletop. Show the actual place or a truthful, specific experience, and make the alternative clear without relying on a paragraph of copy. A single strong scene may work better than a literal split screen.

**Requires:** rights to any destination, talent, or event imagery and care not to promise an itinerary or amenity that is not included. This source documents a video campaign, not a tested static ad or the performance of one frame.
