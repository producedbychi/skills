# Software: Canva, “Really, Really Good Design”

[See the campaign and outdoor example on Canva's newsroom](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/uk-brand-campaign-2025/).

**Offer and visual mechanism.** Canva sells a design tool, but the campaign shows a small sandwich business changing its logo, menu, and social presence. The outdoor example and Debbie & Sons assets make the software's effect visible through the customer's work. The story gives the tool a job beyond displaying an interface.

**Adapt when:** the software changes something customers can show: a menu, report, schedule, storefront, or finished design. Choose one real output and make its improvement legible. If the interface itself is the differentiator, show a truthful, readable screen instead.

**Requires:** approved customer work or an honestly labeled illustration; accurate product capabilities; permission for any real customer's identity. Do not invent a client transformation or turn a staged example into a testimonial. The campaign page documents the creative, not the conversion rate of its individual outdoor image.
