# Education app: Duolingo's five-second Super Bowl spot

[See the campaign frames and creative process in Figma's case study](https://www.figma.com/blog/the-anatomy-of-duolingos-super-bowl-ad/).

**Offer and visual mechanism.** Duolingo needed a lesson reminder to register in five seconds. Its familiar owl became the visual anchor; the eyes direct attention toward the short line “Do your Duolingo.” The team refined expression, framing, and letter shape so a single frame could communicate the idea. This is a motion campaign, but its still-frame discipline transfers to static ads.

**Adapt when:** the brand has a character, person, or symbol that already means something to its audience. Let that asset carry the action, then use a short line to clarify what to do. If the brand has no recognized mascot, inventing one for a single ad may create confusion.

**Requires:** rights to the character and accurate use of the brand's visual system. Borrow the relationship between focal point and action, not Duolingo's owl or joke. The case study reports push-notification delivery timing, not a static image's conversion performance.
