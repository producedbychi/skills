# Public-interest cause: UNICEF and UNFPA's 2026 campaign guide

[See the campaign's design and photo examples in its published style guide](https://www.unicef.org/media/178046/file/Style_Guide_for_6_Feb_2026.pdf).

**Offer and visual mechanism.** The campaign against female genital mutilation uses a forceful central symbol and collage that pairs advocates' faces with images of action. Its guide calls for urgency without losing the role of sustained investment and collective action. The visual is not only a distress cue; it gives the viewer a way to understand what change demands.

**Adapt when:** a cause needs to connect human stakes to a specific, truthful action. Use imagery with consent and context, then make the requested action and its role clear. A quieter cause may need dignity, progress, or community agency rather than this campaign's urgent treatment.

**Requires:** ethical image use, documented claims, and accurate representation of the people and place shown. Never fabricate a beneficiary story or use trauma as decoration. This is an official design guide, not evidence that a particular static execution converted donors.
