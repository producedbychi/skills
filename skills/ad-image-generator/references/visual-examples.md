# Visual library

Use this index only when a visual choice needs help. Pick one or two cards by the problem they solve, not just the offer category. A service might use a product example's use-moment logic; a product might use a service example's visual metaphor. If the direction is already clear, skip the library.

Each card explains a creative decision, its asset needs, and its limits. Adapt the mechanism to the user's brand and brief. A user may explicitly ask to borrow a reference's style, but do not make any example the default layout. The two original images are bundled for offline use. The other cards link to published work; if a source is unavailable, rely on the written analysis and do not claim to have inspected it during the current run. None of these examples proves that the exact image increased conversions.
