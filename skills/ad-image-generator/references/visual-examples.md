# Finished visual examples

Open the image that matches the task. These original images were generated for fictional teaching brands. They demonstrate composition choices, not measured campaign performance or layouts to copy. The product package is invented for this example; use the real supplied package for an actual brand. These are complete social ad concepts, not clean Google responsive image assets.

## A service without a portfolio image

![Northline Studio conceptual product-film ad](service-finished.jpg)

Northline sells short product films to independent food makers. The benefit is making product detail noticeable. The large service headline establishes what is being sold before the food image can be mistaken for a bakery ad.

The close crop gives the bread texture room to read. A dark film strip runs into the scored loaf, connecting film and food in one surreal construction. The dominant condensed headline and warm paper palette fit this fictional studio's graphic identity. The strip's curve connects the type area to the incision; it has a compositional job beyond decoration.

Adapt the relationship between service and customer subject. A camera on a desk would identify equipment but say less about what the food maker receives. Another studio might need a different medium, palette, or customer subject. Keep its offer and identity in control.

The result mixes photographic bread texture with a paper-like interior. It does not meet a strict flat-paper-cut brief. Its impossible film strip is a conceptual device, not a literal production method or client-work sample. The small action line needs more size or weight for feed use if retained in the image; alternatively, let the placement's native CTA carry the action. This example teaches service clarity and composition, not exact medium compliance.

## A product in a relevant setting

![Fieldnote Tea editorial product ad](product-finished.jpg)

Fieldnote's fictional message is an evening tea ritual. The headline names that moment, then the pouch and cup make it concrete. The pouch is large and unobscured. Its flat label, folds, and contact shadow give it weight on the table; the cup explains use without hiding the product.

Warm side light reveals paper, ceramic, and wood as different materials. Dark green serif type reads against the quiet wall. The package shares the headline's color, so branding is part of the composition. This is a deliberately calm treatment. A playful or practical brand may need a different pace and type style.

Adapt the use moment, material detail, and reading order. Do not infer sleep or health benefits from the evening setting. For a real product, use the actual package and check its label against the source. This generated package does not demonstrate asset fidelity. For a Google responsive image asset, produce a separate version without the added headline, studio-style wordmark, or action line, and supply those elements through the appropriate fields.
