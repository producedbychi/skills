# Agreements and scope

Use this guide for an estimate, scope of work, agreement draft, or change order. Start from the studio's approved template when one is supplied.

## Estimate and scope

Capture the project objective, included deliverables and versions, technical formats, milestones, client inputs, review rounds, price basis, expenses, exclusions, and assumptions. Match the deliverables to the current creative and execution plan. Do not treat a proposed option as included work.

## Agreement draft

Carry confirmed scope and commercial terms into the approved template. Surface missing decisions about cancellation or postponement, revisions and overages, payment timing, delivery conditions, project files and intermediate materials, confidentiality, credit, liability, dispute venue, and ownership or license of the final work. Do not choose legal positions for the user. If no template is available, make these an explicit term sheet or visibly provisional draft for counsel to review.

## Change order

State the original scope, exact change, effect on deliverables, schedule and price, approval owner, and whether work may begin before approval. Do not present an unapproved change as binding. Keep the original terms visible so the client can see what changed.
